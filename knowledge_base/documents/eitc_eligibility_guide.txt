EITC Eligibility Requirements - Detailed Guide

EARNED INCOME TAX CREDIT (EITC) ELIGIBILITY CHECKLIST

To claim the Earned Income Tax Credit, you must meet ALL of the following requirements:

1. EARNED INCOME REQUIREMENT
You must have earned income from:
- Wages, salaries, tips
- Self-employment income
- Union strike benefits
- Long-term disability benefits (before minimum retirement age)
- Nontaxable combat pay (if you elect to include it)

Investment income (interest, dividends, capital gains) must be $11,000 or less for 2024.

2. SOCIAL SECURITY REQUIREMENT
- You must have a valid Social Security Number issued before the due date of your return
- Your spouse (if filing jointly) must also have a valid SSN
- Qualifying children must have valid SSNs

3. CITIZENSHIP/RESIDENCY REQUIREMENT
- You must be a U.S. citizen or resident alien for the entire tax year
- You cannot file Form 2555 (Foreign Earned Income Exclusion)

4. FILING STATUS REQUIREMENT
- You cannot file as "Married Filing Separately"
- You must file a federal income tax return

5. QUALIFYING CHILD REQUIREMENTS (if claiming credit for children)

For each child, ALL of these must be true:
- Relationship: Child, stepchild, foster child, sibling, or descendant
- Age: Under 19 (under 24 if full-time student), or any age if permanently disabled
- Residency: Lived with you in the U.S. for more than half the year
- Joint Return: Did not file a joint return (except to claim refund)

6. INCOME LIMITS (2024 Tax Year)

Maximum AGI for EITC eligibility:
- No children: $18,591
- One child: $49,084
- Two children: $55,768
- Three+ children: $59,899

7. DISQUALIFYING FACTORS
You cannot claim EITC if:
- You (or spouse) lived outside U.S. for more than half the year
- You are claimed as a dependent on someone else's return
- You have foreign earned income and file Form 2555

SPECIAL RULES:
- Military personnel: Combat pay can be included if elected
- Clergy: Housing allowance is not earned income for EITC
- Disability: Long-term disability may qualify before minimum retirement age

Remember: These are general guidelines. Tax situations vary. Use the IRS EITC Assistant or consult Publication 596 for specific guidance.

Last updated: 2024 Tax Year
Source: IRS Publication 596
